Impacts on Our Oceans

1. Plastic Pollution

Plastic waste pollutes our oceans, harms marine animals and damages underwater ecosystems.

2. Ocean Acidification

Increased carbon dioxide makes seawater more acidic, threatening coral reefs and marine life.

3. Overfishing

Overfishing reduces fish populations, disrupts food chains and threatens marine biodiversity.
